About

Muhammad Rafay Waqar is a policy analyst and researcher specialising in geopolitical risk, international economics, and regional affairs across the Middle East, North Africa, and South Asia — two of the most strategically consequential regions shaping the current global order. He holds a B.A. in Political Science with a Minor in Technology Management from the University of California, Davis (Class of 2026).

His analytical foundation was built at the highest levels of multilateral finance. At the International Monetary Fund, he conducted macroeconomic and financial policy analysis for the OEDMD constituency — representing Algeria, Ghana, Iran, Libya, Morocco, Pakistan, and Tunisia on the IMF Executive Board — working under Executive Director Bahador Bijani and Senior Advisor Saifullah Dogar. At the World Bank Group, he supported the Executive Director's office across work programs spanning 8 MENA countries, contributing to country-level programmes and the Pakistan Country Strategy Framework 2024. These roles provided direct exposure to how geopolitical and economic pressures interact at the institutional level across deeply different political economies.

A published analytical writer since 2018, he has authored 12+ op-eds and features across The News International, The Express Tribune, and Dawn — examining global food security, Pakistan's water crisis, the Uyghur question, and the broader dynamics of political economy and international affairs. His multilingual profile — spanning English, Urdu, Punjabi, Hindi, French, and Persian — enables deep regional research across the MENA and South Asia corridors.

Beyond institutions, Rafay has demonstrated leadership through civic impact. He founded Water4Pakistan, crowdfunding $5,000 to deliver clean drinking water to children in rural Punjab, and served as elected International Students Representative at ASUCD — UC Davis's student government — advocating for over 6,000 international students and securing institutional partnerships across campus.

He is currently building a public research portfolio focused on Pakistan's macroeconomic vulnerabilities and MENA geopolitical risk, and is actively seeking roles in geopolitical risk analysis, policy research, and international economics at think tanks, consulting firms, and international organisations.
